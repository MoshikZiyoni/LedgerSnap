MoneyThumb provides desktop-based conversion software for Windows and Mac. While it produces valid .QBO files, the software requires local desktop installation, annual license fees exceeding $150+, and lacks modern cloud OCR for scanned or rotated PDF archives.

Why Generic ChatGPT & Gemini Fail at QBO Conversion

Many business owners attempt to upload PDF statements into consumer ChatGPT or Gemini. This fails for three critical reasons:

  • No Native OFX/QBO Output: Large language models output text or tables. They cannot compile binary Intuit Web Connect structures with authentic INTU.BID headers.
  • Number Hallucinations: In statements with multi-line payees, LLMs frequently transpose debit and credit columns or misread 0.00 balances.
  • Client Privacy Violations: Uploading confidential non-public personal financial records into public AI training data can violate GLBA privacy compliance.

For compliant conversions, use a certified deterministic engine like LedgerSnap.

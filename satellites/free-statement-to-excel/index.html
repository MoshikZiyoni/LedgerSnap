Why Copy-Pasting from PDF into Excel Fails

Bank statements use complex tabular columns that cause copy-paste failures:

  • Split Rows: Multi-line payee text spills into the transaction amount column.
  • Flipped Signs: Credits and debits are merged into a single unsigned column, breaking balance formulas.
  • Corrupted Dates: Formats like "OCT 14 26" are not recognized by spreadsheet sum functions without manual cleanup.
